Skin Treatments

Skin diseases can be physically uncomfortable and emotionally distressing. Dr. Anuja Bharti provides accurate diagnosis and modern treatment protocols tailored to your unique skin type.

Conditions Treated:

Acne & Black Spots

Occurs when hair follicles plug with oil and dead skin cells. We treat it using targeted topical creams, chemical peels, and laser therapy to clear the skin safely.

Psoriasis & White Patches

Autoimmune conditions causing rapid cell buildup or pigment loss. Treated with specialized phototherapy and clinical medications to manage symptoms effectively.

Eczema, Allergy & Hives

Allergic reactions causing itchy, inflamed, and red skin. Treatment involves identifying triggers, prescribing antihistamines, and soothing topical ointments.

Ringworm & Fungal Infection

Highly contagious fungal infections that thrive in warm, damp areas. Cleared using advanced oral and topical antifungal medications.

Warts, Moles & Freckles

Benign skin growths or sun-induced pigment spots. Safely removed or lightened using precise laser therapy, cryotherapy, or minor surgical excision.

Chickenpox Marks

Stubborn scars left behind after a chickenpox infection. Smoothed out using microneedling, laser resurfacing, and specialized scar-reduction gels.

Itching, Measles & Leprosy

Range from mild allergic itching to severe infectious diseases. Requires comprehensive medical diagnosis and specific long-term antibiotic or soothing care.

Hair Treatments

Hair loss can be caused by genetics, stress, or nutritional deficiencies. We offer a holistic approach including medication, specialized serums, and advanced therapies like PRP to stimulate follicles.

Conditions Treated:

Scalp & Beard Hair Loss

Gradual thinning or patchy hair loss often caused by genetics or hormonal imbalance. Addressed with minoxidil, nutrient supplements, and advanced clinical therapies.

Dandruff Solutions

Flaking of the scalp caused by dry skin or a yeast-like fungus. Treated effectively using prescription-strength medicated shampoos and scalp soothing treatments.

Premature Greying

Greying of hair before the typical age, usually linked to genetics, stress, or nutritional deficiency. Managed through nutritional support and targeted hair serums.

PRP Therapy (Platelet-Rich Plasma)

An advanced treatment where your own blood plasma is injected into the scalp. It stimulates dormant hair follicles and significantly boosts natural hair growth.

Hair Fall & Thinning

Overall reduction in hair volume due to environmental stress, diet, or lifestyle. We provide customized holistic care plans to strengthen roots and reduce shedding.

Face & Cosmetic

Achieve your desired aesthetic with our safe and effective cosmetic procedures. From skin brightening to anti-aging, our clinical approach ensures natural-looking results with minimal downtime.

Conditions Treated:

Dark Circles

Pigmentation or thinning skin under the eyes due to fatigue, aging, or genetics. Rejuvenated using specialized under-eye peels, dermal fillers, and brightening creams.

Unwanted Facial Hair (Laser Hair Reduction)

Excessive hair growth due to hormonal issues like PCOS. Safely and permanently reduced using advanced, painless laser technology.

Skin Glow Therapy

Dull, tired skin lacking radiance. Restored using medical-grade facials, chemical peels, and mesotherapy to deeply hydrate and revive your natural glow.

Anti-aging Treatments

Fine lines, wrinkles, and sagging skin resulting from collagen loss over time. Reversed using Botox, fillers, and collagen-stimulating laser treatments.

Freckles & Sun Spots

Flat brown marks caused by overexposure to UV rays. Effectively faded using Q-switch lasers, intense pulsed light (IPL), and protective skincare regimens.

💅

Nail & Specialty Care

Nail health is often an indicator of overall health. Fungal infections and structural nail issues require specialized, long-term care plans which our clinic expertly provides.

Conditions Treated:

Nail Loss & Infection

Loss or damage of the nail plate due to trauma or severe bacterial infection. Treated with specialized medical care to promote healthy, infection-free regrowth.

Cracked Heels, Palms & Soles

Severe dryness causing deep, painful fissures in thick skin. Healed using heavy medical emollients, keratolytic creams, and lifestyle modifications.

Fungal Nail Treatment

Thickened, discolored, and brittle nails caused by deep fungal growth. Cleared using long-term oral antifungals and targeted medicated nail lacquers.

Detailed Nail Diagnostics

Complex nail deformities or discoloration requiring precise medical diagnosis. We perform detailed clinical assessments to identify underlying systemic or local issues.
